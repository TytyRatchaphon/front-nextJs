# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Readers of web novels and e-books in Thailand. They range from teenagers to working adults looking for entertainment. Context is mostly mobile/tablet on the go, or desktop during free time. Job to be done: discover interesting stories, purchase chapters/books seamlessly, and read without distraction.

## Product Purpose
A premier online fiction reading platform (EnjoyBook). It allows authors to publish and readers to buy and read using various currencies (Coins, Freecoins, Fast Tickets). Success looks like high engagement, easy discovery, and frictionless purchasing.

## Brand Personality
Accessible, engaging, modern, and reliable. (Voice: friendly, encouraging reading).

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, ad-heavy pirate manga/novel sites.
- Sterile, text-heavy academic databases.

## Design Principles
1. Content is King: Book covers and typography should take center stage.
2. Frictionless Commerce: Clear distinction between different currencies (coins, tickets).
3. Clear Hierarchy: Easy navigation through thousands of titles and categories.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Readable typography (sufficient contrast, scalable text for reading).
- Mobile-first responsiveness.
