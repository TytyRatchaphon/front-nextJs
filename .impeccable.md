# Design Context: Enjoybook

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Adults who value efficiency, simplicity, and a clean reading experience.
- **Context**: Users checking progress during a reading session, likely looking for clear, concise information about their status and rewards.
- **Job to be Done**: Understand achievement status quickly and claim rewards with minimal friction.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Sophisticated, calm, and reliable.
- **Tone**: Mature and professional ("Premium Minimalist").
- **3-word Personality**: Elegant, Efficient, Trustworthy.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Redined minimalism. Focus on high-quality typography and intentional white space over decorative elements.
- **Typography**: `Bai Jamjuree`. Use varied weights and negative space to create hierarchy without relying on boxes or heavy borders.
- **Theme**: Light, airy, and high-contrast for maximum readability.

### Design Principles
1. **Efficiency First**: Information should be glanceable. No "hidden" or complex UI states.
2. **Restrained Color**: Use the brand red (`#dc2626`) sparingly for emphasis and success states.
3. **Typographic Hierarchy**: Let the copy do the heavy lifting. Use font size and weight to guide the user.
4. **Subtle Motion**: Use motion only to confirm actions or guide focus, avoiding "bouncy" or distracting effects.
